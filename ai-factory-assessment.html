What we assess

Ten areas that decide whether AI reaches production.

The assessment is tailored to your priorities. Choose the areas that matter most and we focus the working sessions there.

  • 01

    Enterprise AI readiness

    Strategy, sponsorship, skills and operating model needed to move beyond pilots.

  • 02

    Agentic AI opportunities

    Where bounded, human-supervised agents can safely take on real work.

  • 03

    Data architecture and integration

    Data quality, lineage, access and integration patterns that AI depends on.

  • 04

    AI security and governance

    Policies, controls, evaluation and evidence for trustworthy AI.

  • 05

    Legacy application modernization

    Paths for turning brittle systems into composable, AI-ready services.

  • 06

    Palantir Foundry and AIP enablement

    Opportunities to build on Foundry ontologies and AIP for operational AI.

  • 07

    Cybersecurity automation

    Where AI can accelerate detection, response and continuous monitoring.

  • 08

    Regulatory compliance

    Mapping AI use to frameworks such as NIST AI RMF, NIST SP 800-53 and CMMC.

  • 09

    AI infrastructure and operational costs

    Hosting, model and run-cost considerations for sustainable scale.

  • 10

    Production deployment readiness

    Release gates, monitoring and support needed for mission-grade operations.
