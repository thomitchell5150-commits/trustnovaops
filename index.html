The real problem

AI is not just another feature.

Most organizations do not have an AI problem. They have a platform problem. Their products use separate databases, business concepts are modeled differently, and workflows depend on manual interpretation.

Adding a chatbot does not fix that. TrustNovaOps establishes the shared intelligence layer that allows data, applications, people, and AI agents to operate from the same governed understanding.
