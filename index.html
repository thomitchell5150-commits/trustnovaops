Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it actually take to go from zero to CMMC Level 2 certified with TrustNovaOps?
Most customers see meaningful compliance improvement within 2–4 weeks of connecting their stack, and reach audit-ready status in 6–10 weeks. Timeline depends on your starting point — organizations with significant gaps may need longer remediation periods. Our Compliance Scanner provides a precise readiness estimate on day one.
Does TrustNovaOps replace our need for a C3PAO auditor?
No — a C3PAO (Certified Third-Party Assessment Organization) is still required for CMMC Level 2 certification. TrustNovaOps dramatically reduces what that auditor will find and the time they need on-site, by ensuring your evidence is complete, current, and organized before they arrive. Most customers cut their C3PAO engagement time by 60–80%.
How long does implementation take? Do we need a consultant to set it up?
No implementation consultant required. TrustNovaOps uses API-based connectors to integrate with your existing tools (AWS, Azure, GitHub, Okta, etc.). Most organizations are connected and running their first compliance scan within one hour. The platform guides you through setup with a step-by-step onboarding flow.
What makes TrustNovaOps different from Drata, Vanta, or Secureframe?
Those are excellent general-purpose compliance platforms, but they are not purpose-built for defense. TrustNovaOps is designed exclusively for the defense industrial base: it maps natively to CMMC Level 2 and NIST 800-171, integrates with DevSecOps pipelines out of the box, and is the only platform with a dedicated AI system ATO workflow (Agent 007) that addresses the 8 AI-specific risk categories defined by NIST AI RMF 1.0 and DoD guidance. We also publish our pricing transparently — most competitors require a sales call to get a number.
Is our CUI data safe inside TrustNovaOps?
Yes. TrustNovaOps is SOC 2 Type II certified and FedRAMP authorization is in process. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We use read-only API access to connected systems and never store CUI content — only metadata and compliance evidence artifacts. Enterprise customers can deploy on-premise or in AWS GovCloud for complete data sovereignty.
Do we need all 8 agents, or can we start with just a few?
The Starter plan includes 3 agents (Compliance Scanner, Infrastructure Guardian, and Access Controller), which is sufficient for organizations beginning their CMMC journey. The Professional plan unlocks all 8 agents including Agent 007 ATO Streamliner, WARDEN data protection, and the Pipeline Orchestrator for DevSecOps teams. You can upgrade at any time without data migration.
