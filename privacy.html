Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how TrustNovaOps (“TrustNovaOps”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you visit www.trustnovaops.com, use the interactive demonstrations, or contact us. The website is operated by [OWNER TO PROVIDE: registered legal entity name], [OWNER TO PROVIDE: business mailing address].

This policy covers the website only. Any services we provide under a separate agreement are governed by that agreement.

Information we collect

Information you give us through forms

We collect only what you choose to submit:

  • AI Factory Assessment requests: first and last name, business email, organization, and your primary area of interest (required); and optionally job title, industry, organization size, current AI maturity, business challenges, preferred contact method, phone number (only if you prefer a call) and additional information.
  • Contact, discovery call, partnership, investor, privacy and security inquiries: name, email, inquiry type and message; optionally organization, job title, preferred contact method and phone number.
  • Guided demo requests: name, work email, company, workstation count, and optionally job title, primary concern, demonstration interests and notes.
  • Your choices: your acknowledgment of this policy and whether you separately opted in to marketing communications.

Please do not submit classified information, Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), protected health information (PHI), credentials, or other sensitive operational data. Our forms screen for some of these and will ask you to remove them.

Campaign attribution

If you arrive through a link containing campaign parameters (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term), we keep those values, the first page you landed on, and the referring website’s domain in your browser’s session storage. They are sent to us only if you submit a form, so we can understand which channel led to your inquiry. They are deleted when you close the browser tab.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider records standard technical information for every request — such as IP address, browser user agent, requested page and time — to deliver the site, keep it secure and diagnose problems.

Analytics (only with your consent)

If analytics is enabled on this site and you opt in, we use Google Analytics 4 to measure page views and a limited set of interactions (for example, clicks on an assessment button or launching a demonstration). We configure it so that event data excludes names, email addresses, form contents and free text, and we disable advertising features and Google signals. If you do not opt in, analytics scripts are not loaded. You can change your choice at any time on the Cookie Preferences page. We honor the Global Privacy Control browser signal as an opt-out.

Browser storage

See Cookie and Tracking Preferences for the complete list of cookies and browser storage the site uses.

AI demonstration data

The interactive demonstrations (NOVA Platform, ACCORD Cloud ISSO and WARDEN) run in your browser using simulated data and fictional organizations. Information you enter into them — including questions to the ACCORD assistant, control and POA&M edits, and spreadsheets you import — is processed locally in your browser and is not transmitted to TrustNovaOps or to any AI model provider. ACCORD saves your edits in your browser’s local storage so they persist between visits; you can remove them with the demo’s reset function or by clearing site data in your browser.

Do not enter real system, customer, or sensitive data into the demonstrations.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry and, where requested, arrange an assessment, discovery call or demonstration.
  • To evaluate and prepare a proposed AI Factory Assessment.
  • To operate, secure and troubleshoot the website, including filtering spam.
  • To understand which pages, demonstrations and campaigns are useful (aggregated, and only with consent where analytics is used).
  • To send marketing communications, only if you separately opted in.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Marketing communications and opting out

Submitting a form does not sign you up for marketing. We send marketing emails only if you tick the separate, optional marketing box. Every marketing email will include a working unsubscribe link and our postal address, and we keep a record of opt-outs so that you are not contacted again. You can also opt out at any time through the Data Privacy Contact page.

TrustNovaOps has not started email marketing campaigns at the time of this draft.

Data retention and deletion

We keep inquiry records for [OWNER TO PROVIDE: retention period for inquiry and lead records] after our last interaction, unless a longer period is required by law or you become a customer, after which the applicable agreement governs. You may ask us to delete your information at any time; we will do so unless we are legally required to keep it. Opt-out records are kept so we can honor your choice.

Data security

The website is served only over HTTPS with HTTP Strict Transport Security, uses security headers, and pins third-party scripts with integrity checks. Form submissions are stored with our hosting provider and accessible only to authorized TrustNovaOps personnel. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, which is why we ask you not to send sensitive data through the website. See our Security page for more.
