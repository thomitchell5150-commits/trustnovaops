Purpose

This policy describes acceptable use of the TrustNovaOps website and its interactive demonstrations. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

You must not

  • Enter classified information, CUI, PHI, personal data of others, credentials, secrets or real system data into the demonstrations or forms.
  • Present demonstration outputs, scores or generated packages as real compliance evidence, certifications or authorization documentation.
  • Attempt to gain unauthorized access, probe, scan or test the vulnerability of the website, except through good-faith security research that follows our Security guidance.
  • Interfere with or overload the website, including denial-of-service activity.
  • Submit forms automatically or in bulk, or submit false, misleading or spam inquiries.
  • Upload malicious files, including to the spreadsheet import in the ACCORD demonstration.
  • Scrape or harvest content or contact information in a way that violates our robots.txt directives or applicable law.
  • Use the website in violation of any law, including export control and sanctions laws.

Enforcement

We may block access, remove submissions or take other appropriate action in response to violations, and may report unlawful activity to authorities.

Reporting misuse

Report suspected misuse or security issues through the security contact form.
